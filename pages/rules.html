Rules

Official rules

Please read fully before registering. Registration implies acceptance of these rules.

1. Eligibility

  • Open to students, developers, freelancers, and tech enthusiasts, primarily from Agartala and Tripura, but open to online participants elsewhere.
  • Participants must be able to independently use a laptop/desktop with a stable internet connection and a working webcam/mic for Google Meet.
  • Each participant must register individually — one person, one registration, one submission.

2. The 2-hour challenge

  • A website-building challenge brief is released at the official start time and is the same for all participants.
  • Participants have exactly 2 hours from release to build and submit their project.
  • Work must begin only after the brief is released — pre-built templates or pre-written solutions for the specific challenge are not allowed.

3. Claude usage rules

  • Claude (Anthropic's AI assistant) is the required AI development tool for this challenge.
  • You may use Claude for planning, code generation, debugging, content writing, and design suggestions.
  • You remain responsible for understanding, testing, and being able to explain your own submission — AI-assisted does not mean unattended.
  • Using Claude does not imply official endorsement by Anthropic; see our About page.

4. Permitted resources

  • Publicly available documentation, open-source libraries, and your own prior general-purpose code snippets (not prepared for this specific brief) are allowed.
  • Stock images/icons from free, properly licensed sources are allowed; always credit where required by the license.
  • Collaborating with, or submitting work built by, another person is not allowed.

5. GitHub requirements

  • Each participant must create a new public GitHub repository for their submission.
  • The repository must include a clear README describing the project, setup steps, and a live demo URL.
  • Commit history should reflect genuine work during the challenge window.
  • Never commit API keys, secrets, or credentials to your repository — see our FAQ for safe practices.

6. Plagiarism and cheating

  • Submitting pre-built or copied projects (from yourself or others) as if built during the window is strictly prohibited.
  • Claiming authorship of another participant's or a third party's substantial creative/code work is prohibited.
  • Suspected plagiarism will be investigated by the organizing team and may result in disqualification.

7. Submission deadline & late policy

  • Submissions (GitHub repo link + live demo link) must be made through the official submission form before the 2-hour window closes.
  • A short grace period (as announced during the event) may be given for technical/network issues, at the judges' discretion.
  • Submissions received after the deadline and any grace period will not be judged, except in documented cases of platform-wide outage.

8. Disqualification

Participants may be disqualified for: plagiarism, team submissions, abusive conduct toward organizers or participants, submitting offensive or illegal content, attempting to manipulate seat/judging systems, or violating any rule above. Disqualification decisions are final.

9. Refund policy

Please see our full Refund & Cancellation Policy for cancellation windows and conditions.

10. Responsible AI usage

Use Claude responsibly: do not attempt to generate harmful, illegal, or plagiarized content, and respect Anthropic's own usage policies while using the tool during the event.

Rules may be refined for clarity before the event; material changes will be announced to all registered participants via email/WhatsApp.
