Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you register, we collect your full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, age, city, education/profession, GitHub profile URL, experience level, and your consent/terms acceptance status.

Why we collect it

  • To manage your registration, confirm your seat, and issue a unique participant ID.
  • To communicate event details, reminders, and the Google Meet/WhatsApp group links.
  • To evaluate eligibility, run judging, and issue participation/winner certificates.
  • To contact prize winners for disbursal.

How we store and protect data

Registration data should be stored securely by the organizing team's backend system (not this public front-end) with reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data sharing

We do not share your personal data with unrelated third parties. Aggregated, non-identifying statistics (such as total registrations) may be shared publicly or with sponsors.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your registration data by contacting us at hello@agartalaaihackathon.example, subject to any data we are required to retain for legal, financial, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use minimal, privacy-respecting analytics to understand traffic patterns. No personal data from the registration form is used for advertising purposes.

Children and minors

Participants under 18 should have guardian awareness of their participation. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "last updated" date.
