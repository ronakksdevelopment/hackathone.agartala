Prize eligibility

  • Only submissions received before the deadline (including any announced grace period) are eligible.
  • Participants must have registered individually and completed payment/registration confirmation.
  • Winners must be reachable via the contact details provided at registration for prize disbursal.
  • Disqualified submissions (see Rules) are not eligible regardless of score.

Winner announcement process

Winners are announced live during the closing segment of the event (around 9:00 PM IST) and are later published on our Results page along with their project links, once verified by the organizing team.

Cash prizes are disbursed via UPI/bank transfer within 7–14 working days of the event, subject to winners completing a short verification step. See FAQ for details.
