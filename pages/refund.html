Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Cancellation by participant

  • Cancellations made at least 72 hours before the event start time are eligible for a full refund minus any payment gateway processing fee.
  • Cancellations made within 72 hours of the event start time are non-refundable, as the seat may not be re-fillable in time.
  • No-shows on the event day are not eligible for a refund.

Cancellation or rescheduling by organizers

If the organizers cancel or reschedule the event for any reason, registered participants will be offered either: (a) a full refund, or (b) a transfer of registration to the rescheduled date, at the participant's choice.

How refunds are processed

Eligible refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–14 working days. Please contact us with your participant ID and registered email/phone to request a refund.

Disqualification

Participants disqualified for rule violations (see Rules) are not eligible for a refund.

Contact for refund requests

Email hello@agartalaaihackathon.example or message us on WhatsApp with your registration details.
