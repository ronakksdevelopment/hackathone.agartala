About

A transparent, independent hackathon

Built for Agartala's students, developers, freelancers and tech enthusiasts.

Our purpose

We created this hackathon to give the Agartala tech community — students, early-career developers, freelancers and hobbyists — a hands-on, time-boxed challenge that reflects how AI tools are actually used in real website-building work today. It's a chance to practice, learn, and get recognized in two focused hours.

Who can participate

Anyone based in or connected to Agartala/Tripura (and beyond, since the event is fully online) is welcome: college students, school students (with guardian awareness), working professionals, freelancers, and self-taught developers. No prior hackathon experience is required — only a laptop, internet connection, and a GitHub account.

Individual participation only

This hackathon is designed as a solo challenge. Each participant registers, builds, and submits independently — there are no teams. This keeps judging fair and ensures everyone's AI-assisted process can be evaluated on equal footing.

AI-powered website building

Participants use Claude as their AI development partner to plan, design, code, and debug a website within the time limit. The goal is not to test typing speed, but how effectively you can direct an AI tool to produce a working, polished product under real constraints.

Who's organizing this

The Agartala AI Website Hackathon 2026 is organized independently by a small community team based in Agartala, Tripura, with the goal of promoting practical AI literacy and web development skills among local students and developers.

This is an independent community event. It uses Claude as the required AI development tool for the challenge and GitHub as the required submission platform, but it is not officially hosted, endorsed, or sponsored by Anthropic or GitHub unless explicitly stated otherwise with verified written authorization. We will update this page transparently if that status ever changes.

All prize money, judging, and logistics are managed directly by the organizing team. Contact details and escalation paths are listed on our Contact page.
